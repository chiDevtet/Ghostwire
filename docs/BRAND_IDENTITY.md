# GHOST WIRE — Brand Identity

**Status:** Direction locked; final logo artwork and trademark clearance remain outstanding.

## 1. Brand foundation

**Name:** GHOST WIRE  
**Tagline:** Your money. Your business. Your privacy.

GHOST WIRE should feel like a premium financial technology company built on Solana—not a generic DeFi dashboard and not a cartoon-themed crypto product.

### Brand attributes
- Premium, not flashy.
- Futuristic, not cluttered sci-fi.
- Mysterious, not intimidating.
- Trustworthy, without exaggerated security claims.
- Consumer-friendly and credible for business users.
- Confident, clear, understated.

## 2. Positioning

A private payments application for people and businesses who want more confidentiality when sending and receiving supported digital assets. Sell an approachable payment experience, not cryptographic jargon.

## 3. Visual direction

**Locked palette direction:** near-black/graphite, silver, white, electric violet.

Violet is an accent, not a universal fill. Keep the interface predominantly black/graphite. Use violet for primary actions, selected navigation, brand signal treatments, focus states, and privacy status accents. Use semantic colors consistently.

## 4. Logo concept

Create a distinctive abstract **Ghost Mark** combining:
- a simplified ghost-like silhouette or negative-space shape;
- a continuous wire/signal line;
- a small number of connection points or transmission cues.

The mark must be distinctive and legible at favicon and app-icon sizes.

### Logo family
1. Primary lockup: symbol + GHOST WIRE wordmark.
2. App icon: standalone square-safe symbol.
3. Monochrome: white/silver or black version for receipts/documents.
4. Premium campaign emblem: optional dimensional marketing treatment, not the default product logo.

### Avoid
- Cartoon ghost faces.
- Skulls.
- Generic shield-and-padlock symbols.
- Busy circuit-board illustrations.
- Excessive gradients, glows, or tiny details.
- Typography that becomes illegible at small sizes.

Final artwork should include editable source files plus SVG and transparent PNG exports as appropriate. Generated concept imagery is not final production artwork without review.

## 5. Typography

- **Space Grotesk:** display headings and selected brand typography.
- **Inter:** body text, labels, and controls.
- **Geist Mono** or equivalent: wallet addresses, transaction references, tabular numeric details.

Use accessible contrast and readable sizes. Avoid mono type for long prose.

## 6. Brand voice

Confident, direct, clear, and slightly mysterious. Never confusing, alarmist, or over-technical.

Examples:
- Submitted: “Your payment was submitted.”
- Pending: “Waiting for network confirmation.”
- Confirmed: “Transmission complete.”
- Received: “You've received a payment.”
- Wallet connected: “Your wallet is connected.”
- Privacy initialized: “Private payment functionality is ready.”

Use “Transmission complete” only after confirmed execution and reconciliation.

## 7. Messaging hierarchy

Primary: **Your money. Your business. Your privacy.**

Supporting:
- “Send privately. Receive confidently.”
- “Financial privacy, simplified.”
- “Private payments, powered by Solana.”

Always describe the actual confidentiality model. Avoid absolute claims such as “untraceable,” “invisible,” or “completely anonymous.”

## 8. Launch checks

Before public launch:
- Trademark/confusingly similar-name review.
- Domain availability and ownership.
- Social handle availability.
- App-store naming if native apps are later pursued.
- Logo similarity review.
- Asset licensing and editable source-file retention.

This document records selected direction, not legal clearance or availability verification.
